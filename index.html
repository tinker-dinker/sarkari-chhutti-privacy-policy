Privacy Policy for Sarkari Chhutti

Effective Date: August 4, 2026 | Last Updated: August 4, 2026

Sarkari Chhutti ("the App") is an offline-first utility application designed for Indian Central Government employees. We are strictly committed to user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled by the application.

Summary: Sarkari Chhutti does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you input stays strictly on your local device.

1. 100% Offline & Local Storage

The App operates entirely without cloud infrastructure or external server connections:

  • All personal records—including Date of Birth (DOB), Date of Joining (DOJ), custom leave balances, and Leave Travel Concession (LTC) selections—are stored locally on your device inside an application sandbox (via SQLite and SharedPreferences).
  • We do not upload, back up, sync, or transmit your personal data to any external server or third-party service.

2. Information Collection and Sharing

Because the App operates offline, we collect zero personal data, usage metrics, or diagnostic analytics.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address to access any app feature.
  • No Analytics SDKs: The App contains no tracking frameworks (such as Firebase, Google Analytics, or Flurry) or advertising networks.

3. System Permissions

The App requests minimal system permissions strictly necessary to execute local utility tasks:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required only if you enable LTC lapse alerts or service milestone reminders. All notification schedules are processed locally using Android's native WorkManager. No internet connection is used.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13, as no data collection occurs within the application.

5. Updates to This Policy

If updates are made to this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published here with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of the page.
