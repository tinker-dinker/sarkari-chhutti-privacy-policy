Privacy Policy for Sarkari Chhutti

Effective Date: August 5, 2026 | Last Updated: August 5, 2026

Sarkari Chhutti ("the App") is a privacy-focused utility tool designed for Indian Central Government employees. We are strictly committed to user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how user information and device network access are handled by the application.

Summary: Sarkari Chhutti does not collect, track, or share any personal data. All user inputs (leave logs, retirement details, LTC choices) stay strictly on your device. Network access is used solely to download public annual holiday list updates.

1. Local Storage & Privacy

The App operates on a local-first architecture:

  • All personal records—including Date of Birth (DOB), Date of Joining (DOJ), custom approved leave entries, and Leave Travel Concession (LTC) selections—are stored locally on your device inside an application sandbox (via SQLite and SharedPreferences).
  • We do not upload, back up, sync, or transmit your personal data, profile settings, or leave history to external servers.

2. Network Access & Holiday List Updates

The App requests standard internet permissions (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) for a single, limited utility purpose:

  • Public Data Updates: Network access is used solely to fetch updated annual gazetted and restricted holiday JSON files from a public server repository.
  • Zero Data Transmission: During network requests, no user identifiers, personal profile data, device information, or usage analytics are transmitted or recorded.

3. Information Collection & Analytics

Because the App does not collect personal information, we gather zero usage analytics or diagnostic identifiers.

  • No User Registration: You do not need to register, log in, or provide email addresses or phone numbers to use any feature.
  • No Analytics SDKs: The App contains no tracking frameworks (such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or Flurry) and no advertising networks.

4. System Permissions

The App requests minimal permissions strictly necessary to deliver local utilities:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used only to trigger local reminders for upcoming LTC lapse deadlines and career milestone anniversaries. All scheduling runs locally via Android's native WorkManager.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

Any updates made to this Privacy Policy will be published directly to this page with an updated revision date.
